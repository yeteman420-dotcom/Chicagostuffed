The Vibe

Griddle smoke, neon glow.

Chizzy's is downtown San Antonio's late-night detour to somewhere warmer. Chrome counters, a pink-and-aqua glow spilling onto the sidewalk, and a menu that treats the cheesesteak as a starting point, not a rulebook.

Every sandwich is pressed, griddled, and wrapped to go — built for a fast lunch crowd and a slower midnight one.
