How Nationwide Shipping Works

  • Frozen fresh: pizzas are baked, then quickly frozen to lock in the taste.
  • Dry ice: packed in foam coolers with enough dry ice to stay cold for 3 days of transit.
  • Shipping included: standard shipping is included in all pack prices. No checkout surprises.
  • On arrival: if still frozen or cool to the touch, freeze immediately and bake within 90 days.
  • Gifts: add a gift message right on the box, and ship to multiple people in one order.
