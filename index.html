Track your foot's alignment

A guided photo scan estimates how your big toe joint is tracking, using the same angle podiatrists reference on an X-ray — captured from your phone instead.

What you'll need
  1. A hard, flat floor — no thick rugs
  2. A sheet of A4 or Letter paper (optional, gives you a size reference)
  3. About a minute, standing with full weight on the foot you're scanning
