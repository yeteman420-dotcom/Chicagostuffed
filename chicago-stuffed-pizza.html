Born on the South Side of Chicago, our Stuffed Deep Dish is the real deal — two layers of hand-pressed butter crust encasing a generous filling of whole-milk mozzarella, fresh spinach, and fennel-spiced Italian sausage, then blanketed in our slow-simmered San Marzano tomato sauce.

Every pizza is assembled by hand in our Chicago kitchen, flash-frozen to lock in freshness, and shipped directly to your door. All you need is an oven and 45 minutes.

Crust: Enriched wheat flour, butter (cream, salt), water, yeast, sugar, salt, olive oil.

Filling: Whole-milk mozzarella (pasteurized milk, cheese cultures, salt, enzymes), Italian sausage (pork, fennel seed, garlic, red pepper), fresh spinach, garlic, olive oil.

Sauce: San Marzano crushed tomatoes, olive oil, fresh basil, garlic, salt, black pepper, dried oregano.

Contains: Wheat, Milk, Pork. Produced in a facility that handles tree nuts and soy.

  • Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
  • Remove pizza from all packaging. Do not thaw before baking.
  • Place frozen pizza on center oven rack.
  • Bake 40–45 min until crust is golden and cheese is bubbling.
  • Rest 5 minutes before cutting. Use a sharp serrated knife.
  • Internal temperature should reach 165°F.

Oven temps vary. Do not use microwave. Convection ovens: reduce to 350°F and check at 35 min.

All pizzas ship frozen via 2-day air in an insulated foam cooler with dry ice. Orders placed before 12PM CT Monday–Thursday ship same day.
